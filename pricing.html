GOOD TO KNOW

Pricing questions.

How does payment work?

We accept JazzCash and Easypaisa for payments in Pakistan, and NayaPay for international clients. The payment schedule is agreed with you in writing before work begins.

How many revisions are included?

We review the work with you as it progresses and make reasonable revisions. The scope of revisions is confirmed in your quote.

How long will my project take?

Your project is delivered in a minimum of 7 and a maximum of 10 days. We confirm the exact timeline once we understand your requirements.

Can I contact you after launch?

Yes, you can contact us with questions after launch. Any ongoing maintenance or support is agreed with you in your quote.

Who owns the code and the website?

Ownership terms are confirmed in writing before the project starts, so you know exactly what you receive.
